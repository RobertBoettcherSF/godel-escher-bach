# Backlog

Items 1 to 3 are in this release. The gallery lists the rest, in this order, as closed chapters.

1. **MIU blocks** — done in v0.1. Four rules, the I counter, the path, undo, four languages.
2. **Endless steps** — done in v0.2. Each step closes half the remaining gap. The steps are coloured blocks in one bar and never fill it.
3. **Figure and ground** — v0.4. Triangles at the bottom grow into interlocking birds. A slider sets every tile to the same stage. Tap a bird to light its whole set.
4. **Nesting** — pictures inside pictures. Coming soon.
5. **Crab canon** — a line that can be read forwards and back. Coming soon.
6. **Ant fugue** — many small walkers, one large pattern. Coming soon.
7. **BlooP and FlooP** — loops that stop, and loops that might not. Coming soon.
8. **A line about itself** — a sentence or a program that mentions its own writing. Coming soon.

Out of scope until a later item asks for them: music, quotations, scans, and any image taken from the book.
